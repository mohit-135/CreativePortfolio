02 / ABOUT

The person behind the timeline.

I'm Mohit — a multi-disciplinary creative who's spent the last eight years moving between the edit bay, the design grid, and the viewfinder. I like projects that don't sit still in one medium, because most good stories don't either.

My work usually starts with a client's raw footage, a rough brand idea, or a blank memory card, and ends with something that feels considered, frame by frame. I've cut brand films for independent labels, built identity systems for small studios, and shot events, portraits, and products in between set-ups. The throughline is pacing — knowing when to hold a shot, when to cut, and when to leave white space.

"Good editing is invisible. Good design is inevitable. Good photography just waits for the right second."
